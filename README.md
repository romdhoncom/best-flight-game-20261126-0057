# Northwind Mail — Alpine Air Post

Fly the Kestrel, a single-prop STOL bush plane, through a seeded alpine valley: a river, pine forests, snow-capped ridges and four village airstrips. Three sorties with different goals — deliver the morning mail to two villages, thread eight golden rings over the passes and come home, or carry medicine up to Eagle's Rest in a dusk crosswind on a short tank. Lift needs speed, the wing stalls, flaps and gear matter on landing, fuel burns and wind pushes your ground track.

Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the best flight game" bounty, $500 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. Arrows fly (Up/Down pitch, Left/Right bank), W/S throttle, F flaps, G gear, C camera (chase/cockpit), M mission, P pause, R restart — or hold the mouse button and steer with the pointer.
